# Stakeholder Interview Guide

## Purpose

Understand stakeholder workflows, challenges, content requirements, and success measures without collecting unnecessary personal information.

## Eight Neutral Questions

1. How do you currently manage and share information about your business or properties?
2. Which parts of your current workflow feel the most time-consuming or challenging?
3. Who typically needs to access the information you share or publish?
4. What information is most critical for that audience?
5. What process, if any, do you follow before content is released or made public?
6. How do you measure the success or impact of the information you share?
7. What accessibility, browser, or device-related requirements should a platform accommodate?
8. What technical constraints, workflow changes, or limitations would make adopting a new platform difficult for your team?

## Interview Rules

- Obtain instructor approval before outreach.
- Obtain participant consent before collecting notes.
- Do not collect sensitive personal information.
- Do not use automated calling, bulk outreach, or scraped contacts.
- Clearly label simulated interviews.
