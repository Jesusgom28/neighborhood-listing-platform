# Stakeholder Personas

These personas are simulated working hypotheses, not verified representations of actual people.

## Persona 1 — Property Professional

**Name:** Alex (fictional)  
**Role:** Real Estate Listing Agent

**Goal:** Maintain accurate and organized property listings.

**Potential Challenges:**
- Repeated manual updates
- Incomplete listing information
- Difficulty reviewing information before publication

**Proposed Needs:**
- Structured listing form
- Required field validation
- Draft and approval process

## Persona 2 — Local Sponsor

**Name:** Jordan (fictional)  
**Role:** Local Business Owner

**Goal:** Promote a business to people exploring the surrounding neighborhood.

**Potential Challenges:**
- Unclear promotion submission requirements
- Inconsistent business information
- Limited control over publication status

**Proposed Needs:**
- Sponsor submission form
- Preview of promotional content
- Approval status visibility

## Evidence Limitations

Both personas were developed from project assumptions and a simulated scenario. Actual workflows and priorities require stakeholder confirmation.
